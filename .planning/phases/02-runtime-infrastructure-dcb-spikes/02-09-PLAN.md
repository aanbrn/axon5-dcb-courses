Conflict diagnosis on both paths (rejected in `appendEvents` in memory, in `commit()` on Axon Server): walk the cause chain to `AppendEventsTransactionRejectedException`, re-source with `SourcingCondition.conditionFor(marker.position(), criteria)`, record the first non-terminal event as the conflicting event, then re-fail with the original failure.
